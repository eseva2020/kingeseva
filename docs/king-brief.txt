Build a production-ready, premium, highly visual, cinematic business website/PWA

The website must NOT look like a generic CSC portal, MeeSeva copy,
government website, WordPress template, or ordinary local services website.

==================================================
SIGNATURE LOGO EXPERIENCE
==================================================

Create a signature animation called:

"ROYAL DIGITAL AWAKENING"

The website should begin with a premium cinematic logo reveal.

Do NOT create a long splash screen.

Total duration:
approximately 2.5 to 3.5 seconds maximum.

ANIMATION SEQUENCE:

STEP 1 — DARK ENTRY

Start with a near-black / midnight navy screen.

A subtle warm golden ambient light slowly appears behind the center.

STEP 2 — GOLD RING REVEAL

Reveal the outer circular border of the KING emblem.

Do NOT simply fade it in.

Create the impression of a fine golden light traveling around the circular ring
and revealing it progressively.

The effect should look like polished metal catching moving studio light.

STEP 3 — LION REVEAL

Reveal the lion using elegant directional gold light sweeps.

The lion should emerge from darkness in layers:

mane → face → jaw → lower mane.

Avoid:
particle explosion,
fire,
lightning,
gaming effects,
aggressive glow.

STEP 4 — CROWN ACTIVATION

Reveal the crown with a subtle downward settle motion.

The crown should feel physically present.

Use very small overshoot / spring easing.

STEP 5 — DIGITAL ENERGY PULSE

When the crown settles:

send one restrained golden pulse through the emblem.

The pulse travels:

Crown
↓
Lion
↓
Circular frame

STEP 6 — CYAN AWAKENING

Activate the digital cyan details.

Lion eye:
one elegant cyan glow pulse.

Crown gems:
soft cyan illumination.

Background circuit patterns:
barely visible subtle activation.

The lion eye must NOT blink continuously.

STEP 7 — BRAND REVEAL

Reveal:

KING
e-Seva Services

Design KING as the dominant word.

If creating an associated wordmark,
place a premium crown accent above the letter "I".

Then reveal:

మీ అందరి సేవలో

Use premium typography and controlled spacing.

STEP 8 — HERO TRANSITION

Instead of ending the animation and loading another screen,
transform the intro directly into the website hero.

The emblem should smoothly reposition into the hero composition.

No visible page reload.
No hard cut.
